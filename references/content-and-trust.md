# NexAI Content and Trust Rules

Visual consistency is insufficient if claims, evidence, AI behavior, or certification language drift.

## 1. Core product loop

Use this causal chain in product copy and information architecture:

`目标 / 起点 → 真实任务 → 项目交付 → 过程证据 → 反馈 / 复核 → 我的 AI 护照 → 机会 → 结果反馈`

The smallest meaningful value unit is:

`一个真实任务 + 一个可检查产物 + 一组可信证据 + 一次明确成长`

## 2. Claim model

Every high-value capability claim should make these questions answerable:

- Claim: what exactly is being claimed?
- Evidence: what observable artifact supports it?
- Task: in what real task was it demonstrated?
- Rubric: against what criteria was it evaluated?
- Interpretation: what does the result mean, and what does it not mean?
- Reviewer: AI pre-review, peer, mentor, employer, or named certification reviewer?
- Recency: when was the evidence produced or reviewed?

## 3. Evidence levels

Do not collapse all evidence into one “verified” badge.

- Self-reported: user-entered history or manually uploaded material.
- Source-linked: linked repository, deployment, document, or account with provenance.
- Process-supported: includes history, milestones, decisions, and contribution evidence.
- Reviewed: assessed against a rubric by a disclosed reviewer.
- Certified: formal high-trust conclusion with named human assessor and governance.

AI may help classify or pre-review evidence. AI alone cannot create a formal certification conclusion.

## 4. P/C/M/I independence

Keep these dimensions independent:

- P — Provenance: source and chain of custody.
- C — Contribution: what the person actually did, including AI/collaborator roles.
- M — Mastery: current demonstrated ability.
- I — Impact: real effect or outcome.

A GitHub link proves neither originality nor mastery by itself.

## 5. Certification language

Required:

- explain the project/deliverable;
- explain theory/practical assessment;
- explain rubric/weights where available;
- name or identify the human review role;
- state the exact credential output;
- distinguish completion from passing certification.

Forbidden unless independently proven:

- “国际标准认证”;
- university degree equivalence;
- UNESCO/OECD/ISO endorsement;
- guaranteed employment or salary;
- automatic certification by AI;
- “verified original author” based only on a link or upload.

When referencing external frameworks, say “参考并对齐” rather than “获得背书”.

## 6. AI interaction and consent

- AI must not imply it silently reads GitHub, social accounts, files, or private history.
- Account connections and uploads are voluntary and explicit.
- Explain what information will be used and for what purpose.
- Consequential external actions need user confirmation.
- Public sharing is opt-in.
- Lightweight ability checks are advisory, not certification.

## 7. Job and market data

- State the source or source page and date when possible.
- Use ranges, not false precision, unless the source provides exact values.
- Add: “薪资与需求会随市场变化，以实时招聘信息为准.”
- Job demand is context, not a promise.
- Certification can improve expression of evidence but does not guarantee hiring.
- The user controls application and career decisions.

## 8. Partner and logo claims

- Only show a partner logo when a real relationship or source requirement exists.
- Do not infer partnership from curriculum similarity or a public reference.
- Keep partner logos visually secondary to NexAI identity.
- Use supplied official files; never recreate a partner mark.

## 9. Copy style

- Plain, concrete Chinese.
- Lead with verb + outcome: 完成、交付、证明、连接、沉淀、复核.
- Avoid inflated abstractions such as “重塑未来” without a concrete task.
- Avoid excessive English labels. Keep necessary product terms such as Skill, MCP, RAG, Workflow, Multi-Agent when they are the accurate technical vocabulary.
- Use `AI Builder` to mean a person who works with AI to turn ideas into real-world value.
- Brand mission: `Empower Every AI Builder` / `成就每一位 AI 创造者`.

## 10. Required disclaimers by scenario

| Scenario | Minimum disclosure |
|---|---|
| AI pre-review | AI review is advisory; final high-trust conclusion requires human review |
| External upload | source and authorship are self-reported until verified |
| Job figures | source/date and market-change disclaimer |
| Certification | passing criteria, reviewer role, exact credential output |
| Account connection | explicit consent and what data is read |
| Public portfolio | user chooses what becomes public |
