# NexAI Page Patterns

Use these patterns as topology. Preserve content truth and adapt density to the user task.

## 1. Global information architecture

Primary user-facing channels in the current product shell:

1. 首页
2. AI护照
3. 项目
4. 活动
5. 社区
6. 机会
7. 专业认证

Strategically, these condense into four families: 我的 AI 护照、社区、学习、机会. Do not rename individual visible channels without a broader IA decision.

## 2. Home aggregation

Required order:

`Banner → AI护照 → PBL实战项目 → 精选活动 → 专业认证 → 机会`

- AI Passport appears immediately below the banner.
- Opportunity appears immediately after professional certification.
- Each section needs one message, representative content, and one quiet link to the full route.
- Avoid adding full detail-page content to home.

## 3. Project listing

- Intro states that projects create demonstrable, reviewable work.
- Filters cover role/category and difficulty.
- Cards show image, level, role/category, title, short outcome, partner/source if real, and detail action.
- Do not mix retired project content into the current Vibe Coding sample.

## 4. Project detail

Topology:

1. back/breadcrumb;
2. dark immersive hero;
3. level, duration, project title, outcome statement;
4. `开始实战` primary CTA and secondary example action;
5. image/video panel;
6. value/outcome summary;
7. detailed overview/tasks/evidence/review;
8. sticky summary/enrollment card on desktop.

Content semantics:

- project brief;
- real deliverable;
- process evidence;
- human review where required;
- AI Passport deposition;
- next opportunity.

## 5. Project follow room

Full-screen route, outside the standard app content layout.

Desktop:

- left learning/task rail;
- middle operation canvas;
- right output rail/pane collapsed by default.

Left rail order:

1. compact progress + collapsible directory;
2. teaching video;
3. expandable transcript, about 100 characters by default;
4. complete current task, options, file, and `让 AI 开始实操` action;
5. previous/continue controls.

Middle rules:

- blank before the user acts;
- operation results only afterward;
- composer stays available;
- no repeated task guidance or progress.

Right rules:

- tabs: 预览 / 文件 / 证据;
- collapsed by default;
- opening a relevant result may expand it;
- evidence explains what was saved and what remains unverified.

## 6. Activities and competitions

- Intro is text-only: no framed hero/image.
- Immediately below: compact image-led carousel titled `最受欢迎`, message anchored upper-left.
- Then activity list.
- Distinct `赛事` section after activities, with its own filters and cards.
- Orange may identify deadlines or active enrollment.

## 7. Community

- Parchment-gray workspace, white feed cards, purple identity/actions.
- Two-column layout: primary feed + right recommendation rail.
- No internal left channel rail.
- Topic filtering uses a compact horizontal control.
- Header contains recommendation/following tabs only.
- Preserve legacy gallery as an archive route; do not overwrite it during IA changes.

## 8. My AI Passport

- Compact heading: `我的 AI 护照` plus one explanatory sentence on one line when space permits.
- No English eyebrow and no edit-mode button.
- Wide desktop: free direct-manipulation board.
- Intermediate: readable single-column flow, drag/resize disabled.
- Mobile: stacked cards and companion overlay.
- First permanent card: system-generated AI 能力卡.
- Cards distinguish platform-reviewed evidence from self-reported/external material.

## 9. Opportunity

- Lead with what evidence makes the user relevant to the opportunity.
- Job/project cards must separate live facts from assumptions.
- User controls applications and external submissions.
- Show source/date context for market figures.

## 10. Professional certification detail

Career-proof pattern:

1. dark image-led outcome hero;
2. compact proof strip;
3. sticky anchor navigation;
4. concrete outcomes;
5. learning process;
6. explicit modules + learning modes;
7. one real capstone project and deliverables;
8. assessment and human review;
9. exact credential outputs;
10. switchable related roles and capability requirements.

For current L3 AI 应用开发 content:

- AI 认知与 Prompt 工程;
- VibeCoding 工程化实战;
- AI Agent 开发;
- 电影票务智能体 Demo + 项目说明文档;
- 课程学习 → 项目实训 → 理论考试 → 专家评审 → 获得认证;
- 50% 项目答辩, 30% 方案与工作文档, 20% 在线知识测试, 75 分通过;
- L3 岗位认证主书 + 方向专项能力证明 + 企业导师评审结论;
- related roles with change disclaimer.

## 11. Responsive transformation

Do not interpret responsive design as scale-down.

- Desktop side-by-side hero → mobile image-backed single-column hero.
- Four-column proof strip → two columns on intermediate → one list on mobile.
- Card grids → fewer tracks → one column.
- Sticky anchor nav → horizontal scroll strip.
- Side panel → overlay/collapsed drawer.
- Free board → ordered single-column document flow.
